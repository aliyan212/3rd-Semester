## ⚡ Quick Download Guide for Classmates (No Git Needed!)

If you're here just to get your study materials for an upcoming quiz, assignment, or exam, here are the easiest ways:

### 📥 Method 1: Download a Single PDF or File (Fastest)

1. Navigate to the folder you want above (e.g., `Calculus & Analytical Geometry`, `COAL`, or `Data Structures`).
2. Click on the file you want to open (for instance, `Thomas Calculus 13th [Solutions].pdf`).
3. On the file preview page, look at the top-right toolbar:
   - Click the **`Download`** button (or the icon showing an arrow pointing down into a tray: ⤓ **Download raw file**).
   - Your browser will immediately download the PDF directly to your device!

---

### 📦 Method 2: Download Everything in 1 Click (Whole Repo as ZIP)

Want all textbooks, slides, and notes on your laptop or phone at once?

1. Scroll to the top of this repository page: [aliyan212/3rd-Semester](https://github.com/aliyan212/3rd-Semester).
2. Click the bright green **`<> Code`** button.
3. Click **`Download ZIP`**.
4. Extract the ZIP folder on your computer. You now have the complete offline collection of all subjects!

---

### 📱 Method 3: Reading Notes on Mobile / Browser

All markdown (`.md`) files (such as lecture summaries and code explanations) render automatically in formatted reading mode on any phone or browser. Just tap on any `.md` file to read it like a blog post!

## 🔮 How to Use with Obsidian (Recommended for Studying)

All the lecture notes in this repository are written in Markdown and formatted as an **Obsidian Vault**.

### Why use Obsidian?
- **🕸️ Graph View:** Visualize how Data Structures, Architecture, and Algorithms connect.
- **⚡ Blazing Fast Search:** Search across all semester notes in milliseconds (`Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + F`).
- **🔗 Wikilinks Support:** Jump effortlessly between concepts using internal connections (`[[Topic]]`).
- **📴 100% Offline:** Study anywhere, anytime, without needing an active internet connection.

### Quick Setup:
1. Download and install [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) (it's 100% free for Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS & Android).
2. Download or clone this repository to your computer.
3. Open Obsidian and select **"Open folder as vault"**.
4. Pick the downloaded `3rd-Semester` folder.
5. Enjoy your fully linked, rich student knowledge base!

## 🤝 How to Contribute

Got helpful slides, past papers, lab solutions, or caught a typo in the lecture notes? Contributions are always welcome!

### Easiest Way (Via GitHub Web):
1. Navigate to the folder you want to add files to.
2. Click **`Add file`** > **`Upload files`** (top right).
3. Drag and drop your notes or past papers.
4. Click **`Propose changes`** to submit a pull request!

### Or Reach Out:
Feel free to open an **[Issue](https://github.com/aliyan212/3rd-Semester/issues)** or reach out directly with your materials, and they will be added for the whole class.
